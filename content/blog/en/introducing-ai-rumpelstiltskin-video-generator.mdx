In October 2026, a short AI clip of a little imp tip-toeing through a barn spread across TikTok and X, and people started putting their friends into the dance. <ProjectName /> lets you do that from one photo, without filming or editing anything.

## What it does

You upload one photo of one adult. The AI makes that person the star of a Rumpelstiltskin scene: in a cream tailcoat, they sneak through a hay barn, shush the camera and tip-toe with a sly grin while a friend gasps. You get a video of about 12 seconds, widescreen 16:9 or vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, with original music, as an MP4 you can download and share.

It makes a new video, not a face swap. The AI studies your photo and films a new scene around that person, so the face, body and clothes move together. The shots change from video to video. We don't use the viral clip, its audio or footage from any film.

## How it works

1. Open the generator on the [home page](/en#generator) and pick a photo: one adult, full body or waist up, facing the camera, in good light.
2. Tick the box to confirm it's your photo, or that the adult in it agreed.
3. Choose Standard (10 credits) or Pro (20 credits, sharper 1080p).
4. Sign in with an email link or Google, and buy a credit pack if you need one.
5. Wait a few minutes. The video plays on the page, and we email you when it's ready.

The [step-by-step guide](/en/how-to-make-rumpelstiltskin-video) covers each step and what makes a good photo.

## What it costs

Credits come in one-time packs: 10 credits for $4.99, 20 for $7.99 and 30 for $11.99. There is no subscription and credits never expire. If a video fails, or doesn't finish within 24 hours, its credits go back to your balance automatically. See [pricing](/en/pricing) for the details.

## Use it kindly

Only upload photos of yourself, or of an adult who said yes. No photos of children. Every video is AI-generated, so share it as a joke, not as real footage. Your photo is deleted about 24 hours after upload.
